About Us

Welcome to the Dubai College Lost and Found, your dedicated platform for reuniting students, staff, and visitors with their lost belongings. At Dubai College, we understand how easy it can be to misplace items during a busy school day. Our goal is to make the recovery process simple and efficient for our community, ensuring lost items find their way back to their rightful owners.

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster a supportive and honest environment within the Dubai College community. By providing a secure and reliable lost and found service, we aim to minimize the stress of losing important items, from school materials to personal belongings. Through this platform, we encourage everyone to contribute to a responsible and caring school culture.

Connecting people with their lost belongings across Dubai College.
